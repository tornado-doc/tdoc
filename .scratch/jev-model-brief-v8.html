Jev, explained simply

Full longer version: v5. This page is the short cut — three questions, three visuals. Spec: research-claw.

A normal pretrained LLM that reads your input once and, instead of writing words, scores the options you gave it — trained so the scores are honest probabilities.

1. How is Jev actually built?

Community reconstruction from TypeSafe docs, Archer Hume's API probe, and the open clones that appeared in 48 hours.

The packing recipe was copied in two days. Long context, 255-option scoring, and calibrated stop — those look like data + objective, not the diagram.

2. Are they actually good?

One matrix. All cells measured on one Mac mini (M4, 16 GB) on 2026-09-20; Jev over the network.

Authors' numbers point the same way: kev-4b 0.76 vs Jev 0.86 OOD; Nimble 90.1% vs Jev 93.2% on Nimble's set. Community median speed-up vs chat models was 7× — not the advertised 194×.

Read: short text — good clones match Jev locally for free. Agent-shaped inputs — every clone fails on quality, not just speed. None has a calibrated "I'm done," which is what a loop needs to stop.
