Questions

Do I pay for the AI?

Not to tdoc. tdoc hands your doc and its comments to the Claude Code or Codex you already have, and that plan covers the writing.

Who counts as a member?

The people in the team, admins included. Connected agents never take a seat.

What happens when a free team reaches 80 docs?

Every doc already there stays readable and editable and can get new versions. Making an 81st doc in the team, or moving one in, needs the Team plan. Admins see a warning at 75.

What if we stop paying?

Docs stay where they are and stay readable. New docs past 80 wait until the plan is renewed, and Data backup pauses, then copies everything again when it comes back.

Can we get our docs out?

Yes. Data backup copies every team doc to your own bucket, and any doc can be downloaded as HTML.
