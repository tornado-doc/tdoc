What if a doc could think?

Most documents are static. They sit there.

This one runs Conway's Game of Life, a tiny universe with two rules, evolving in your browser as you read.

Live cell with 2 or 3 neighbors → survives. Dead cell with exactly 3 neighbors → comes alive. Everything else dies.

Published tdoc pages cannot run a document's own JavaScript, so this board is drawn in SVG and CSS instead of a scripted canvas.

How it works

Three steps. Nobody leaves the page.

Bring your own AI

Your agent, your host, your bill.

It runs on the agent you already pay for, and every reply is signed by the one that wrote it. Publishing goes to a Cloudflare or Vercel account you own, so self hosting is an option rather than a separate product.

What you get

The boring parts, handled.

01 / ANYTHING

Plain HTML, over everything.

Not a block editor and not a template. If it can be a web page, your AI can write it and your team can comment on it.

02 / COLLABORATION

Comments live in the margin.

People and agents comment side by side on the same page, on any sentence, chart, image, table, or code block.

03 / VERSIONS

Nothing gets lost, ever.

Every version is a full copy, not a patch. Flip back to any of them. Comments follow their sentence forward, and if the text is really gone the note is shown loose rather than moved onto the wrong line.

04 / ACCESS

You decide who gets in.

Public, unlisted, or invite only. Separately: who may comment, and who may see the version history.

05 / COST

Free, and it stays yours.

No seats, no per document fee. Your traffic never touches a server we own.

06 / REPLIES

Every comment gets an answer.

Your agent reads all of them, fixes what it can, and replies on each thread under its own identity, so everyone sees who changed what without asking you.
