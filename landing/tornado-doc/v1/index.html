Why this is annoying today

Every doc tool was built for people talking to people.

Your AI is not in the room. It is a window you paste into and paste back out of — and every round of feedback, you are the one carrying it.

A document + a chat window

You copy the comments out. You paste the answer back. You remember who asked what. Nobody can see whether their note was handled.

One page

The note and the reply sit on the same sentence. Anyone can see what was asked, what changed, and who did it.

Works with what you already use

Bring the AI you already pay for.

tdoc has no AI of its own and no subscription. It runs on your existing agent, and every reply on the page is signed by the one that wrote it — so you can always see who changed what.

What you get

The boring parts, handled.

Your readers install nothing

They click the link and comment in the browser. One sign-in, and their name is on it. That is the whole ask.

Comment on the picture, not just the words

Charts, diagrams, images, tables, code. Point at the thing you mean instead of describing where it is.

Nothing is lost when the AI rewrites

Comments follow their sentence into the new version. If the text really is gone, the note is shown as unattached — never quietly moved onto the wrong line.

Go back to any version

Every version is a full copy, not a patch. Flip between them, and comments on deleted text are still there.

You decide who gets in

Public, unlisted, or invite-only — and separately, who may comment and who may see the history.

Free, and it stays yours

Publishing goes to a Cloudflare or Vercel account you own, inside free tiers normal use will not reach.

Open source

Own your documents.

There is no tdoc server holding your pages. Publishing deploys to infrastructure in your own account, on a domain you control.
