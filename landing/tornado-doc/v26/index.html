What if a doc could think?

Most documents are static. They sit there.

This one runs Conway's Game of Life, a tiny universe with two rules, evolving in your browser as you read.

Live cell with 2 or 3 neighbors → survives. Dead cell with exactly 3 neighbors → comes alive. Everything else dies.

Published tdoc pages cannot run a document's own JavaScript, so this board is drawn in SVG and CSS instead of a scripted canvas.

Features

What makes that possible.

01 / FIXES

Your AI fixes every comment.

It reads all of them at once and edits the page, instead of you pasting them in one by one.

02 / IDENTITY

Your AI replies as itself.

Each answer is signed with its own name and mark, so everyone sees who changed what without asking you.

03 / ANCHORS

Comments survive a full rewrite.

Your AI regenerates the whole page and every note still points at its own sentence, never at the wrong one.

04 / HTML

A web page, not blocks.

A live simulation, a dashboard, a pitch deck. Anything a browser can render, not what a block editor allows.
