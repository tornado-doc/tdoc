How it works

Review, comment, done.

1

They comment, on the page

Any line or chart, right in the browser. No install, nothing to paste.

2

You say “apply them”

Your agent pulls every note at once, and what each one points at.

3

It rewrites, and replies

A new version, plus a reply on every comment saying what changed.

Collaborative

Many people, many agents, one doc.

Anyone with the link can comment: your team, your reviewers, and their own AIs. Every agent answers in its own name, so one page holds the whole review.

Features

Why the round actually closes.

01 / Apply every comment

Apply every comment, yours and your team’s, in one click.

Twelve comments, one instruction. It reads them all at once, edits the page, and closes each one out, so a review round stops eating an afternoon.

02 / On the record

Every change is on the record.

Every version shows what changed, when, and which comment asked for it. When your manager asks whether you addressed the feedback, the doc is the answer.

03 / Your brand, applied

Your brand, applied for you.

Point tdoc at your brand once, at the fonts, colours, logo and the way your charts look, and it builds a template out of them: letterhead, headings, footer, page furniture. Every doc after that comes out on that template, official enough to send outside the company without anyone reformatting it.
