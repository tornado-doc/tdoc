Where does it get published?

To a free Cloudflare account you own. The first time, your agent walks you through it: you click create account and enable R2 in your own browser. No card. About five minutes, once.

Only want it on your machine? Say keep it local and skip the account entirely.

Readers need no account to read. Commenting asks for a one time GitHub sign in, so every note carries a name.

What can be commented on?

Any sentence you can select, and any picture, chart, table, code block, diagram or live widget on the page. A comment sticks to the thing it points at, not to a line number, so it survives a rewrite.

3

Tell your AI to fix the comments.

You never copy notes back and forth. It reads every comment and what each one points at.

Read the comments on my doc and fix them.

It edits the page, publishes a new version, and replies to each comment saying what changed. The reply is signed by the agent that made the change, so whoever commented finds out without chasing you.

Every version is kept.

A new version never breaks the link you already shared.

A comment left on v2 stays on v2, and says so.

Use the version picker in the top bar to flip back. Comments stay anchored to the version they were left on, so you can see what the page looked like when someone objected.

5

Share it the way you want.

Anyone with the link can read. You choose who can comment.

UnlistedAnyone with the link can read. Never listed anywhere.Default
PublicThe same, and may be listed.
PrivateYou, plus the accounts you allow.

Hit Share in the top bar for the link. Owners also get visibility and commenting controls there.

Who can see it?

Unlisted is the default: anyone with the link can read, and it is never listed anywhere.

Public is the same, and may be listed.

Private is you plus the accounts you allow.

6

Take it with you.

Your doc is a plain HTML file, and it stays one.

DownloadThe rendered page as a file you own.
DuplicateYour own copy of someone else's doc, to edit.
Copy as MarkdownThe text, with or without the comment thread.

All three are in the top bar. Nothing here locks your writing inside tdoc.
